# Proposal

## Why
Render an original bounded farm with freely moving gardeners and no interior obstacles.

## What Changes
- A welcoming open garden.
- Integrate Blender art and render uniquely colored gardeners with readable names.
- Implement keyboard, simultaneous touch, pause and focus-safe input.
- Verify WebGPU rendering, movement, bumping and desktop/mobile layouts.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `garden-world`: A welcoming open garden.
### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact
Application source, verification and documentation. No breaking changes to other games.
